I like taking an idea far enough to find out whether it works. Sometimes that means building a model or simulation; sometimes it means benchmarking GPUs, reverse engineering a robot, or figuring out why a promising ML model falls apart outside a notebook. This site is where I write about those investigations.

I have a degree in electrical engineering and have spent the past four years building backend and data systems at Red Dog Media. More recently, my work has included production machine learning, pricing experiments, and causal inference. I'm especially interested in the gap between a model that looks good in evaluation and a system that makes useful decisions in practice.

Outside work, I follow questions across math, software, and hardware. I've explored the constraints of a hexagonal puzzle, simulated competing advertisers, and tested local language models on a three-GPU machine. I enjoy projects where building something changes my understanding of the problem, even when the original idea turns out to be wrong.

I'm interested in work involving learning, optimization, and computing systems, especially where I can investigate an open question and help turn the answer into something that works. If you're working on something along those lines, I'd like to hear about it.
